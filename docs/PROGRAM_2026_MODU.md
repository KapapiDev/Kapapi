## 2. General/technical track funnel

Published scale:

- applicants → **1R: 8,000**
- 1R → **2R: 1,000**
- 2R → **3R: 200**
- final → **5**

### 1R selection

1R is an **idea-document review**.

Three responsible mentors from the selected mentor institution evaluate the challenge application, including idea differentiation/effectiveness and institution-specific focus indicators.

Institution T/O also reflects application volume, so evaluation-fit alone does not determine selection probability.

### 2R selection

1R participation/activity results are evaluated through documents such as the business plan, startup-activity report and mentoring report.

### 3R selection

2R finalists enter a regional public IR process where prototype results and future business plan are evaluated.

## 4. KAPAPI 1R strategy

The 1R goal is **not** to pretend KAPAPI is already a mature marketplace.

The goal is to prove that the idea is worth validating.

Application framing priority:

1. real professional-work capacity/deadline problem
2. clear GM target and situation
3. existing-platform workflow friction
4. KAPAPI mechanism: QUEST + price × delivery-time BID
5. strong long-term differentiation: outcome-oriented orchestration
6. realistic short-term validation plan
7. founder/domain execution ability

Avoid weak framing such as:

- “Korean Upwork”
- “online side-job platform” by itself
- “game-like quest platform” as the main business thesis

Better core framing:

> KAPAPI lets a GM submit a short professional task and receive competing price + delivery commitments, then progressively automates the entire path from work request to completed result.

## 6. Pre-1R prototype target

Build a thin but convincing prototype showing:

- GM creates QUEST
- PLAYER submits price + delivery BID
- GM compares and selects
- PLAYER relevant career/history
- TIME ATTACK
- LEVEL / EXP
- NDA/security concept
- architecture/CAD example

Optional high-value proof:

- one real external PLAYER BID
- external GM interview snippets/results
- 30–60 second demo showing the transaction in one pass

Do not spend pre-1R time on production PG, tax settlement, complex disputes or Autopilot guarantees.

---

## 7. 1R validation objective

If selected, use 1R to generate evidence for 2R:

- external GM interviews
- real QUESTs
- qualified PLAYER supply
- paid transactions where practical
- price/time selection data
- security objections
- completion/on-time/rework data
- first-bid latency and valid BID count
- repeat GM behavior

The strongest later evidence is:

> **external GM + own money + real QUEST + repeat use**

Founder-originated CAD tasks are useful for mechanism testing but should not be presented as primary demand validation.

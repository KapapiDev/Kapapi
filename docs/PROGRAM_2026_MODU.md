# 2026 모두의 창업 프로젝트 — KAPAPI Strategy

Updated: 2026-09-03

## 1. Application strategy

The application should not present KAPAPI as a finished marketplace, a CAD service, a generic “Korean Upwork,” or a magical AI outsourcing system.

The strongest story is the actual founder-origin problem and the growth logic that follows from it.

### Founder-origin story

> 퇴근 후 남는 시간에 할 수 있는 작은 온라인 부업을 찾고 싶었다. 하지만 기존 선택지는 고정 알바, 장기 프리랜서 활동, 또는 내가 먼저 서비스 상품을 만들어 판매하는 방식에 가까웠다. 내가 원한 것은 이미 존재하는 작은 업무를 골라 끝내고 바로 보상받는 방식이었다.

This leads to a task-first market:

```text
work exists first
→ PLAYER chooses suitable QUEST
→ PLAYER commits PRICE + DELIVERY TIME
→ work is completed
→ REWARD + verified history
```

The mirrored GM problem is:

> 회사나 개인에게는 누군가 끝내야 하지만 채용이나 복잡한 외주 절차를 밟기에는 작고 불규칙한 일이 계속 생긴다.

KAPAPI connects unused skill/time with unresolved work.

---

## 2. Differentiation story

Do not sell the idea as “the same marketplace, reversed.”

The differentiation should be explained as a sequence:

1. **Task-first instead of storefront-first** — the work is the primary object.
2. **PRICE × DELIVERY TIME** — fragmented availability and urgency become market information.
3. **Task-specific trust** — completions create evidence about who reliably finishes which kind of work.
4. **KAPAPI recommendation** — the platform reduces comparison burden using earned data.
5. **Routing / recovery** — with enough evidence, KAPAPI increasingly handles selection and failure recovery.
6. **Outcome Layer** — eventually the user increasingly submits work and receives a result without caring which execution resources were used.

Long-term execution may combine human PLAYERs, AI, deterministic automation, specialist partners and hybrid workflows.

---

## 3. What to say KAPAPI is

Short description:

> **카파피는 일이 먼저 올라오고, 할 수 있는 사람이 가격과 완료시간을 제안해 끝내는 온라인 업무 플랫폼입니다. 거래가 쌓일수록 카파피가 어떤 실행 방식이 어떤 업무를 잘 끝내는지 학습해 추천·배정·복구를 자동화하고, 궁극적으로는 일을 넣으면 결과를 받는 업무 실행 레이어로 발전합니다.**

Shorter:

> **작은 업무를 골라 돈 벌 수 있는 Task Market에서 시작해, 일을 맡기면 결과가 돌아오는 Outcome Layer로 성장합니다.**

North Star:

> **해야 할 일을 올리면, 결과로 돌아온다.**

## 4. What not to say

Avoid leading with:

- “건설업 외주 플랫폼”
- “CAD 매칭 서비스”
- “온라인 전문 부업 플랫폼” alone
- “게임처럼 일하는 플랫폼” alone
- “Korean Upwork”
- “AI가 알아서 최고의 사람을 바로 골라준다”
- universal SLA/outcome guarantees

Game/world terminology is a memorable UX layer after the business is understood.

---

## 5. 1R framing priority

Recommended order:

1. **Founder problem** — wanted small bounded online side work, not a storefront or fixed shift
2. **Mirrored GM problem** — small/irregular/urgent unresolved work that does not justify hiring or heavy procurement
3. **Why existing behavior is awkward** — profile/service-first discovery and delegation friction
4. **KAPAPI mechanism** — QUEST + mandatory PRICE × DELIVERY BID
5. **Task-first marketplace proof** — real work moves from unresolved to complete
6. **Data flywheel** — transactions create task-specific trust and liquidity data
7. **Growth path** — recommendation → routing/recovery → repeat capacity → Outcome Layer
8. **Founder/domain execution ability** — architecture/CAD as one credible higher-skill testbed, not market definition
9. **Concrete validation plan** — both PLAYER supply and external paid GM demand

---

## 6. Pre-1R prototype target

The prototype should visibly demonstrate:

- category-neutral task-entry hero
- `작업 찾기` PLAYER entry
- open QUEST feed, not seller storefronts
- ordinary bounded work examples plus one higher-skill founder-domain example
- PLAYER PRICE + DELIVERY BID
- task-specific career/history signals
- KAPAPI recommendation with visible rationale
- GM confirmation
- TIME ATTACK
- result delivery + accept/revise
- restrained LEVEL / EXP
- future path from transaction data → routing → Outcome Layer

Do **not** require universal automatic routing before 1R.

Do not spend pre-1R effort on production payment custody, tax automation, broad category backend, deep dispute tooling or SLA guarantees.

---

## 7. 1R validation plan

### PLAYER validation

Test whether people will:

- browse real bounded QUESTs
- BID without needing a storefront
- trade off PRICE vs DELIVERY according to availability
- complete reliably
- return for more work

### GM validation

Test whether external GMs will:

- submit bounded work they currently do themselves, delay or send to existing vendors
- pay real money
- accept the result
- return with another self-funded QUEST

### Market validation

Track:

- time to first valid BID
- eligible BIDs per QUEST
- PRICE / DELIVERY distributions
- assignment rate
- completion / on-time / revision
- recommendation acceptance/override
- PLAYER effective earnings
- GM management time
- repeat PLAYER / repeat GM

Strongest proof:

> **external GM + real QUEST + real PLAYER + accepted result + real payment + repeat**

Founder-originated CAD QUESTs may validate supply/engine behavior but must not be counted as external GM-demand proof.

## 8. Growth story for judges

```text
Founder cannot find the kind of small side work he wants
↓
Build a market where work exists first
↓
People convert spare time + skill into completed QUESTs
↓
Organizations convert irregular unresolved work into external capacity
↓
Transactions create price, delivery, fit and performance data
↓
KAPAPI recommendations become more reliable
↓
KAPAPI increasingly routes and recovers execution
↓
Repeat businesses use KAPAPI as external capacity
↓
Human + AI + automation + partners produce outcomes
↓
WORK IN → RESULT OUT
```

This makes the long-term ambition credible because each stage creates the evidence required for the next.

## 10. Final application thesis

A strong final thesis is:

> **카파피는 ‘프리랜서를 찾는 곳’을 하나 더 만드는 것이 아니라, 일이 먼저 존재하는 시장을 만듭니다. 초기에는 사람이 자신의 남는 시간과 기술에 맞는 QUEST를 골라 PRICE와 DELIVERY TIME을 제안해 끝내고 보상받습니다. 이 거래가 쌓이면 카파피는 어떤 실행 방식이 어떤 업무를 안정적으로 끝내는지 데이터로 학습할 수 있습니다. 그 결과 추천, 배정, 복구를 점차 자동화하고 장기적으로는 사람·AI·자동화를 조합해 ‘해야 할 일을 올리면 결과로 돌아오는’ 업무 실행 레이어로 발전합니다.**
